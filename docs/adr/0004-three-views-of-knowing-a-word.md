# Three views of knowing a Word: your Status, the Tutor's assessment, and Recall strength

Spaced repetition needs a per-Term schedule, and the Tutor judges your knowledge from your answers. Both could have been folded into Status. We keep Status as your own judgement only, and add two separate views beside it: the Tutor's assessment (moved by Tutor adjustments) and Recall strength (from the Review schedule). None of them sets another; differences are shown and may prompt a suggestion, but you decide.

The schedule is FSRS-style and built only from Reviews (Missed, Hard, Got it), not from Status. Status only decides who takes part: Learning 1–4 Terms are scheduled, Known and Ignored never show as Due.

## Considered Options

- Status-driven intervals (Learning 1 → 1 day … Learning 4 → 3 weeks), with each Review moving the Status: one model, but the Reader would keep overwriting your judgement. Rejected.
- The Tutor keeps nudging Status (as Tutor adjustments did through slice 17): simple, but you couldn't tell your own rating from the Tutor's. Rejected.

## Consequences

- Tutor adjustments, which changed Status until now, move the Tutor's assessment instead. Statuses the Tutor already moved stay as they are and become yours.
- A Term carries three readings of the same scale; the screens must label which is which.
- A Term that Follows Parent shares its Parent's schedule, as it shares its Status.
